Thinknote Privacy Policy

Effective Date: November 25, 2026

Short version: Thinknote is a local-first notes and tasks app. We do not require an account, we do not run ads or tracking analytics, and we do not collect or store your notes on our servers. If you optionally enter your own Anthropic (Claude) or Deepgram API key, your requests are sent directly from your device to those providers.

1. Information We Collect

Thinknote (com.fabri1820.thinknote) does not require user registration and does not collect, transmit, or store personal data on developer-operated servers.

All content you create in Thinknote is stored locally on your device, including:

  • Projects, sections, tasks, subtasks, notes, tags, filters, templates, and snippets.
  • App preferences (such as theme selection and default project/tag settings).
  • AI chat history, custom system prompts, and any optional API keys you choose to enter.

2. Optional Third-Party AI & Voice Services (Bring Your Own Key)

Thinknote works completely offline without any API keys. If you choose to configure optional AI or voice features in AI Settings using your own API keys, the app communicates directly from your device with those third-party providers over encrypted connections (HTTPS / WSS):

  • Anthropic (Claude API): When you send an AI chat message, your message, conversation history, custom system prompt, and any workspace items accessed by enabled AI tools (such as projects, sections, tags, or tasks) are transmitted directly to Anthropic's API to generate a response. See Anthropic's Privacy Policy.
  • Deepgram: When you use optional voice transcription or text-to-speech, live microphone audio or response text is transmitted directly to Deepgram's API to perform speech-to-text or text-to-speech processing. See Deepgram's Privacy Policy.

Your API keys are stored locally on your device and are only sent to their respective provider to authenticate your requests. We never see, log, or have access to your API keys, prompts, notes, or audio.

3. Device Permissions

Thinknote requests the following optional Android permissions:

  • Microphone (RECORD_AUDIO & MODIFY_AUDIO_SETTINGS): Used only when you explicitly tap the voice input button to transcribe speech into text. Audio is never recorded in the background and is not stored on developer servers.

4. Analytics, Advertising, and Data Sharing

Thinknote does not display advertisements, does not use third-party advertising SDKs, does not include behavioral tracking or analytics SDKs, and does not sell or rent your data to any third party.

5. Data Retention and Deletion

Because your data is stored locally on your device, you have full control over it at all times. You can:

  • Edit or delete individual projects, tasks, notes, tags, filters, snippets, and AI chat sessions in the app.
  • Clear saved API keys anytime in AI Settings → API Keys.
  • Erase all local data at once via Settings → Clear All Local Data.
  • Remove all app data completely by uninstalling Thinknote from your device.

6. Children's Privacy

Thinknote is not directed to children under the age of 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

7. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time (for example, if optional cloud sync or new features are added in future versions). Any updates will be posted on this page with an updated effective date.
